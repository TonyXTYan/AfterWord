Pronunciations

Words rewritten before they are spoken, in every line, the voice lab and the say fallback. The longest match wins; a word that begins or ends with a letter or digit only matches whole words (uv leaves uvicorn alone); an all-lowercase word ignores case, one with capitals matches exactly. Profile tables override the global one.
